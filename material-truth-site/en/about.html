About Material Truth

We're not contractors. We're not IDs.
We're the friend who knows materials.

Material Truth was founded by people who've spent years inside the materials supply chain — sourcing, walking factory floors, and helping friends-of-friends check their site. We've seen too many new property owners overpay simply because no one explained the material to them.

Where We Started

It started with "just check this invoice for me"

Our founder started out simply helping a friend "check an invoice" — the friend had been quoted a confusing material list for a renovation and asked us to verify the pricing and specs. When we checked, the same batch of tiles had been marked up by nearly half.

Word spread, and more friends-of-friends started asking the same questions: is this material worth the price? Is the grade being exaggerated? Will I be pressured into signing something? We realised this wasn't a one-off problem — it was an industry-wide information gap.

So we turned "checking invoices for friends" into a proper, transparent service anyone can use — but the starting point hasn't changed: be a friend first, talk business second.
