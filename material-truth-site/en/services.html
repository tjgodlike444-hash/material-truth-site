What We Offer

Six core services

Book a single service, or let us walk you through the whole process end to end.

SERVICE / 01

Material Grade Consultation

We explain the grades, certifications, and durability differences between tiles, marble, flooring, paint and more — so you know exactly what you're buying.

SERVICE / 02

Quotation Review

Already have a quote from a contractor or ID? We'll check whether the material pricing is reasonable or inflated.

SERVICE / 03

Direct Factory / Warehouse Sourcing

Skip the middleman markup — we connect you directly to the factory or warehouse with pricing clearly itemised.

SERVICE / 04

On-Site Verification

We join you on-site to check samples, batch numbers and specs, making sure what's delivered matches the quotation.

SERVICE / 05

Project Budget Planning

We allocate your total budget across material categories, and flag where you can save — and where you shouldn't.

SERVICE / 06

Custom Material Shortlist

Based on your structure and how the space is used, we prepare a tiered, price-attached shortlist of recommended materials.
